pick.mesh is CreativeTrio's pickaxe, from Poly Pizza (poly.pizza/m/cJp88qPPLc),
Public Domain (CC0). Baked by tools/bake_pickaxe.py.

The model ships with a base colour texture, Diffuse_palette_2.jpg — and it is
what its name says: a 256x256 grid of flat swatches, nine across and five down,
with a UV pointing into the middle of one. There is no detail in it to lose, so
the baker samples it once per vertex and the image itself does not ship. This
renderer has no binding to spare for it in any case: sixteen sampled textures of
a guaranteed sixteen are already spent.

One change to the geometry, made in the baker and printed when it runs: the
model is authored with the haft stopping at the underside of the iron and a
separate stub sitting on top, so on the screen the handle reads as stuck under a
lump rather than driven through an eye. The shaft's top ring is carried up to
the iron's crown, which is where a haft belongs and where the stub then becomes
the end of it coming out the other side.

It replaced Kenney's pickaxe — "tool-pickaxe" from the Survival Kit (2.0),
www.kenney.nl, also CC0 — which is a fine model and was drawn badly: the shader
turned its head seventy degrees about the haft, and the head is the whole width
of a model that is only 3.9 cm deep, so it arrived edge-on with its two arms
reading as loose pieces either side of the handle.
